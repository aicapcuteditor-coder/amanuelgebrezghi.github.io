FAQ
Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

How do I pay for my website?
+
After placing your order, I'll contact you within 24 hours to discuss your project and arrange payment. I accept Zelle, PayPal, Cash App, and bank transfer. Payment is split — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.
What if I don't like the result?
+
No worries! Every plan includes revision rounds. I'll keep refining your site until you're completely happy. Your satisfaction is guaranteed — I won't consider the project done until you approve it.
Do you need my passwords or login info?
+
No. I build your website and deliver the complete files to you. You own everything — the code, files, and domain. You never need to share any passwords with me.
Can I update the website myself after delivery?
+
Yes! I deliver clean, well-structured code along with a simple guide on how to update your content. Need ongoing updates? Just email me and I'll help at a small hourly rate.
How will my website be hosted?
+
I'll guide you to set up free hosting on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel — all fast and reliable platforms that host your site for free. I can also help you set up a custom domain like yourname.com.
How long does it actually take?
+
Starter: 1–3 days. Pro: 3–5 days. Business: 5–7 days. Timelines start once I receive all your content and project details. Rush delivery available — just ask!
What do I need to provide to get started?
+
Just describe what you want in the order form! Tell me the purpose of your site, your audience, colors or styles you like, and any example sites you admire. The more detail, the better the result.
