FAQ
Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

How do I pay for my website? +
After you place your order, I'll contact you within 24 hours to discuss your project and share payment details. I accept PayPal, bank transfer, and other methods. Payment is typically split — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.
What if I don't like the result? +
No worries! Every plan includes revision rounds. I'll keep working on your site until you're happy with it. Your satisfaction is my priority — I won't consider a project done until you approve it.
Do you need my login information or passwords? +
No. I build your website and deliver the files to you. You'll own everything — the code, the files, and the domain. You never need to share any passwords with me.
Can I update the website myself after it's done? +
Yes! I deliver clean, well-structured code. I'll also give you a simple guide on how to update your content. If you need ongoing updates, just email me and I'll help at a small hourly rate.
How will my website be hosted? +
I'll guide you to set up free hosting on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel. These are reliable, fast platforms that host your site for free. If you want a custom domain (like yourname.com), I'll help you set that up too.
How long does it really take? +
Starter sites take 1–3 days. Pro sites take 3–5 days. Business sites take 5–7 days. These are from when I receive all your content and approve the project. Rush delivery is available — just ask!
What do I need to provide to get started? +
Just describe what you want in the order form! The more detail the better — tell me the purpose of your site, your target audience, any colors or styles you like, and any example websites you admire. I'll take it from there.
