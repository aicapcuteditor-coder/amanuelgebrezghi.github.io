FAQ
Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Do I pay before or after seeing my website?
+
After! We build your website first, then you get a live preview link in your dashboard. You review it and only pay when you're completely happy. Zero risk.
How do I pay?
+
Once you approve your website, a Pay button appears in your dashboard. We accept all major cards via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Payments are instant and secure.
What if I don't like the result?
+
No problem! Every plan includes revision rounds. We keep refining until you're 100% happy. You don't pay until you love it.
Do you need my passwords or login info?
+
Never. We build your website and deliver the complete files. You own everything. No passwords ever needed.
How long does it take?
+
Starter: 1–3 days. Pro: 3–5 days. Business: 5–7 days. Rush delivery available — just ask!
How will my website be hosted?
+
We set you up on free hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel) and can help you get a custom domain like yourname.com.
What types of websites do you build?
+
We build all types — portfolios, business sites, restaurants, online stores, landing pages, blogs, non-profits, and more. If you can imagine it, we can build it!
